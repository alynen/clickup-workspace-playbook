# Designing pretty ClickUp lists

Five guidelines for lists that look great and stay consistent across a whole workspace. *February 2026*

## 1. Pick the right custom field type

Email, phone, attachment, checkbox, number, dropdown: match the field type to the data. That alone makes a huge visual difference.

## 2. Add icons to field names

Use ClickUp's native emoji picker when you create a custom field. It makes the list prettier and gives people a visual cue while they fill in a task. I use emojis on dropdown options too.

## 3. Use dropdowns wherever you can

Dropdowns deserve their own rule. They have a big visual impact on the list, and they're your best friend when you build dashboards. Look at your text fields and ask: *can I categorize this?* If yes, make it a dropdown.

## 4. Hide the columns you don't use

Not using Assignee, Due Date or Priority? Hide them. Nobody from ClickUp will show up demanding you keep them in your view. I promise.

## 5. Explore grouping

You can group tasks by much more than status: priority, assignee or any custom dropdown. And you can also choose not to group at all.

## ⭐ Bonus: AI dropdowns for outside data

When data comes from outside ClickUp (say, a spreadsheet connected through Zapier) instead of a ClickUp form, it often lands as plain text. Add an AI dropdown that reads that text field and picks the right option, so the list stays tidy without anyone sorting it by hand.
