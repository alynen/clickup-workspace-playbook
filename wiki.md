# Building a company wiki in ClickUp

Lessons from creating our company wiki from scratch. *July 2024*

ClickUp docs marked as **Wiki** get priority in ClickUp AI's answers. That turns a wiki from a place people rarely visit into the knowledge base behind every AI answer in the workspace, which made it worth building properly.

Writing a whole company's wiki can feel daunting. These five steps made it a lot less so.

## 1. Start with the basics

You have to start somewhere, and that's often the hardest part. Create a **Welcome** doc:

- a nice cover and icon
- a banner with a short description of the company
- the purpose of the wiki
- a few headings to give it structure
- a **Quick Access** section with the most important links

## 2. Map what you want to cover

Watch the company's daily routine and note the main areas the wiki needs. Organizing by department is the most common structure, but use whatever fits how your company actually works.

## 3. Document as you work

You don't need to write it all in one sitting, and you won't get the time to anyway. Wikis are living documents. Add content as you work on related tasks, one area at a time.

## 4. Use what already exists

Even brand-new companies have *something* written down somewhere. Improve it instead of starting from zero.

## 5. Let AI help

My favorite tip. I use ClickUp AI every day for first drafts of project scopes, SOP formatting, descriptions and tutorials. And the more the wiki grows, the better ClickUp AI gets at answering questions about your company.
